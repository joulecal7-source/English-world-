📐 Grammar Tips

💡 Imperative (Commands)

Use the verb alone (no subject) to give orders or instructions.

Clean your room. / Brush your teeth. / Stop fighting!

💡 Don't + verb (Negative imperative)

Add Don't before the verb to say what NOT to do.

Don't be late. / Don't stay up too late. / Don't worry.

💡 Can you…? (Polite requests)

Can you…? makes a command softer and more polite.

Can you help me set the table? / Can you pass the water?

💡 Possessive adjectives

My / Your / His / Her / Our / Their show who owns something.

Where is my toothbrush? / Pack your bag. / Show me your grades.
