👤 1. Identite — Qui est qui ?
Structure : Sujet + AM/IS/ARE. AM pour I, IS pour He/She/It, ARE pour You/We/They.
1I am Joule.
Je suis Joule.
2You are Musuwa.
Tu es Musuwa.
3He is Eric.
Il est Eric.
4She is Aline.
Elle est Aline.
5It is the cat.
C est le chat.
6We are John and Jean.
Nous sommes John et Jean.
7They are Brigitte and Aline.
Ils sont Brigitte et Aline.
💼 2. Profession — Quel est ton metier ?
Utiliser AM/IS/ARE + a/an + metier. Ex: I am a teacher.
1I am a teacher.
Je suis enseignant.
2You are a student.
Tu es etudiant.
3He is a doctor.
Il est medecin.
4She is a nurse.
Elle est infirmiere.
5It is a tool.
C est un outil.
6We are workers.
Nous sommes des travailleurs.
7They are engineers.
Ils sont des ingenieurs.
😊 3. Etat et Description
Utiliser AM/IS/ARE + adjectif pour decrire un etat. Ex: I am happy.
1I am happy.
Je suis content.
2You are kind.
Tu es gentil.
3He is tall.
Il est grand.
4She is beautiful.
Elle est belle.
5It is big.
C est grand.
6We are tired.
Nous sommes fatigues.
7They are intelligent.
Ils sont intelligents.
🏃 4. Le Present Continu (Present Continuous)
Structure : AM/IS/ARE + Verbe-ing. Pour une action en cours maintenant.
1I am working.
Je suis en train de travailler.
2You are learning.
Tu es en train d apprendre.
3He is sleeping.
Il est en train de dormir.
4She is cooking.
Elle est en train de cuisiner.
5It is raining.
Il est en train de pleuvoir.
6We are talking.
Nous sommes en train de parler.
7They are playing.
Ils sont en train de jouer.
🕰 5. Le Passe Simple (Was / Were)
Structure : I/He/She/It + WAS et You/We/They + WERE. Pour une action terminee dans le passe.
1I was at home.
J etais a la maison.
2You were late.
Tu etais en retard.
3He was a child.
Il etait un enfant.
4She was here.
Elle etait ici.
5It was cold.
Il faisait froid.
6We were friends.
Nous etions amis.
7They were happy.
Ils etaient heureux.
⏳ 6. Le Present Perfect (Have/Has been)
Structure : I/You/We/They + HAVE BEEN et He/She/It + HAS BEEN.
1I have been busy.
J ai ete occupe.
2You have been helpful.
Tu as ete d une grande aide.
3He has been sick.
Il a ete malade.
4She has been a leader.
Elle a ete une meneuse.
5It has been a long day.
Ca a ete une longue journee.
6We have been together.
Nous avons ete ensemble.
7They have been away.
Ils ont ete absents.
💃 7. Le Passe Continu (Past Continuous)
Structure : WAS/WERE + Verbe-ing. Action qui etait en train de se derouler dans le passe.
1I was dancing.
Je dansais.
2You were eating.
Tu mangeais.
3He was running.
Il courait.
4She was sleeping.
Elle dormait.
5It was raining.
Il pleuvait.
6We were watching TV.
Nous regardions la tele.
7They were laughing.
Ils riaient.
💡 Conseil Teacher Joule : Present : am/is/are. Passe simple : was/were. Passe continu : was/were + ing. Present perfect : have/has been.
